Heap (language stack) overflow bug
Class: Major
Discovered: 20260817
Status: PATCHED (20260817)

This bug allowed for out-of-bounds writes in the allocated heap, specifically
the language call stack area, due to a small piece of older shim code that
hard-coded the number of entries that were allowed to be on the stack at one
time. This was idiomatic at the time, but was never changed when the update
to platform-defined limits was made. This was in platforms c, posix, openbsd
and puredarwin, files platforms/n/main.c. This could either cause outbound
memory writes or a segmentation fault depending on the platform. This was
fixed by simply not hard-coding the limit, instead grabbing it from the
platform limits defined.

Platforms c, posix, and openbsd were patched. Platform puredarwin was
NOT patched, since it is depreciated at the current time of this bug.
